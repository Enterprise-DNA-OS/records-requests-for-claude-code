The registry has no public-records-requests family. This is the request coordination desk, distinct from document storage, records retention and investigative casework. No NZ/AU invoice or measured keyword volume was verified. Buyer fit is the weak score. ## Sources and scope

- [CivicPlus product description](https://www.civicplus.com/nextrequest-public-records-software/): intake, routing, review, redaction, release and invoicing. These are vendor features, not claims that every feature ships in the free base.
- [Requests Report](https://www.civicplus.help/nextrequest/docs/requests-report): selectable fields and CSV/XLSX exports. Our importer accepts the register with explicit column, date and status mapping. A real customer export was not accessed.
- [Document downloads](https://www.civicplus.help/nextrequest/docs/download-your-records): requester downloads, not a guaranteed administrative account archive.
- [Ombudsman official information guidance](https://www.ombudsman.parliament.nz/what-ombudsman-can-help/requests-official-information/make-request-official-information) and [DPMC OIA guidance](https://www.dpmc.govt.nz/our-business-units/cabinet-office/supporting-work-cabinet/cabinet-manual/8-official-information-and-public-records/official-information-act-1982): sources for bounded evidence checks.

The free base does not provide public portals, redaction, delivery, payments or legal decisions. It runs an internal information officer's desk without requiring those services to be connected. Keep existing delivery and document tools until a scoped replacement has been tested. No claim of total feature parity, statutory certification or guaranteed savings is made.
